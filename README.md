# drss-3986-odour-survey
DRSS-3986 Odour Survey Datascape and Fieldwork prototypes

## Latest Preview: V6

- [Datascape](https://andreapickensnz.github.io/drss-3986-odour-survey/DRSS-3986%20Odour%20Survey%20-%20Datascape%20-%20v6.html)
- [Fieldwork](https://andreapickensnz.github.io/drss-3986-odour-survey/DRSS-3986%20Odour%20Survey%20-%20Fieldwork%20-%20v6.html)

V6 reflects RSHL's 11 Nov 2026 decisions: the assessor is inferred from the booking's primary officer, the standard Survey Date replaces the separate question, and minute times are entered manually. Ten fixed rows are shown as requested; platform support for fixed rows remains to be confirmed. The minute block remains at the bottom of the phone form.
